# Privacy Policy - Lucky Block Poster

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Lucky Block Poster is an internal tool operated by Lucky Block Studio. Its only purpose is to upload short videos made by Lucky Block Studio to Lucky Block Studio's own accounts on TikTok, YouTube and Instagram.

Data we access: when the studio authorizes the tool, it receives access tokens and basic account information (account ID and username) for the studio's own accounts. These are used only to publish the studio's videos and are stored as encrypted secrets.

Data we do not collect: the tool has no users other than the studio. It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data of other people, viewers or followers. It does not read messages, comments or contacts.

Retention and deletion: tokens can be revoked at any time from the TikTok, Google or Meta account settings; they are then deleted from the tool.

Contact: luckyblockstudio.games@gmail.com
